# Palworld (Pocketpair): Commercial Performance and Business Story

Notes compiled 2026-11-14. "Official" = Pocketpair / Microsoft / court-document disclosures. "Estimate" = third-party analytics (Alinea, Newzoo, trackers). Many primary pages (Bloomberg, PC Gamer, Automaton, GamesRadar bodies) were paywalled or truncated when fetched, so several figures below come via secondary coverage or search-result summaries; these are flagged.

## 1. Sales, player figures, revenue and profit over time

### Takeaway
Palworld went from 8 million Steam copies in 6 days (Jan 2024) to 25 million players in a month, and by July 2026 Pocketpair reported 40M+ total players while Alinea Analytics estimated ~30.5M copies sold and ~$700M gross revenue, about 89% of copies on Steam. Pocketpair has never published revenue; the only leadership statement on profit is Mizobe's March 2024 "tens of billions of yen" remark to Bloomberg.

### Cited Findings
**Launch window (official unless noted)**
- Early access launch: 19 Jan 2024 on Steam and Xbox (Game Preview / Game Pass). — [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2024/11/pocketpair-details-lawsuit-filed-against-it-by-nintendo-and-the-pokemon-company)
- 8 million Steam copies sold in 6 days (official, late Jan 2024). — [SI Video Games](https://videogames.si.com/news/palworld-8-million-sales-6-days)
- 31 Jan 2024 (official): Steam ~12 million copies sold, Xbox ~7 million players, total above 19 million. — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/palworld-is-the-biggest-third-party-xbox-game-pass-launch-ever-with-over-7-million-players-on-xbox-so-far); [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2024/01/31/palworld-biggest-3rd-party-launch-ever/)
- Microsoft, 31 Jan 2024 (official): biggest third-party Game Pass launch ever (displacing High on Life), 7M+ players across console and Windows PC, peak of nearly 3 million daily active users on Xbox, largest third-party day-one launch on Xbox Cloud Gaming. — [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2024/01/31/palworld-biggest-3rd-party-game-pass-launch-ever/)
- ~22 Feb 2024 (official): 25 million total players in first month: Steam 15 million, Xbox 10 million. Note Pocketpair switched wording from "copies sold" to "players" for Steam here. — [VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/palworld-has-surpassed-25-million-players-in-its-first-month-studio-says)
- All-time Steam peak concurrent players: 2,101,867 (Jan 2024), second-highest in Steam history at the time behind PUBG's 3.25M; highest ever for a paid game. — [GameWorldObserver](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/02/21/palworld-revenue-mau-january-charts-newzoo); [VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/palworld-hits-more-concurrent-steam-players-than-any-paid-game-in-history)
- By 21 Feb 2024, daily peak CCU on Steam had already fallen to ~400k. Pocketpair's John "Bucky" Buckley reportedly had expected about 50k CCU. — [GameWorldObserver](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/02/21/palworld-revenue-mau-january-charts-newzoo)

**Estimates, Jan-Feb 2024**
- Newzoo (Jan 2024 data, 37 markets excluding China and India): highest-grossing premium game in the US and UK for January; 11th by MAU across PC and Xbox; 7.8% of the tracked global game audience played it; average playtime 16 hours. — [GameWorldObserver](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/02/21/palworld-revenue-mau-january-charts-newzoo)
- Back-of-envelope outlet estimate: if most of the ~12M Steam buyers paid full price, Steam gross was ~$360M at end of Jan 2024 (upper bound; ignores regional pricing and the 10% launch discount). — surfaced via [games.gg](https://games.gg/ar/news/palworld-hits-19-million-players/)
- Analysts estimated more than 5 million purchases in China alone (date of estimate unclear, early 2024). — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-45756)

**Latest data (July 2026)**
- Pocketpair (official, just before the 10 July 2026 1.0 launch): 40 million+ total players across Steam, Xbox and PlayStation, roughly 2.5 years after early access. This is cumulative players including Game Pass, not sales. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/survival/over-2-years-since-launch-palworld-hits-40-million-players-right-before-its-1-0-release-and-now-has-over-8-million-more-players-than-pokemon-red-blue-and-green/); [Inven Global](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/23559/palworld-hits-40-million-players-a-milestone-ahead-of-july-10-official-launch)
- One report put the 40M split at 64.9% Steam, 31.1% Xbox (largely Game Pass), 4% PlayStation (I could not confirm which outlet or whether this is official or an estimate; treat as unverified). — surfaced via [nerdschalk](https://nerdschalk.com/palworld-40-million-players-before-1-0/)
- Alinea Analytics (estimate, report dated 22 Jul 2026): ~30.5 million copies sold worldwide and ~$700 million gross software revenue lifetime; Steam 27.1 million copies; 1.0 itself added ~1.5 million Steam copies (~$25M), ~300,000 PlayStation copies and just under 70,000 Xbox premium sales; DAU averaged 3.7 million over the latest weekend; MAU projected above 10 million across PC and console; ~20% of Steam owners (about 5.2M) have 100+ hours, nearly 200,000 have 500+ hours. — [OtakuKart summarising Alinea](https://otakukart.com/palworld-surpasses-30-5-million-copies-sold-hits-700-million-revenue-and-3-7-million-daily-players-after-version-1-0/); [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/palworld-1-0-has-reportedly-sold-over-1-8-million-copies/)
- Source discrepancy: the Alinea write-up says Steam is "about 86%" of copies, but 27.1M / 30.5M is ~89%. — [OtakuKart](https://otakukart.com/palworld-surpasses-30-5-million-copies-sold-hits-700-million-revenue-and-3-7-million-daily-players-after-version-1-0/)

**Profit**
- Mizobe to Bloomberg (March 2024, via secondary coverage): the game cost under 1 billion yen (~$6.7M) and had generated profits in the "tens of billions of yen", which he called "too big for a studio with our size to handle". GamesRadar's wording was "several billions", so secondary sources differ on scale. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/palworlds-made-so-much-money-its-too-big-for-a-studio-with-our-size-to-handle-but-ceo-says-its-not-going-to-go-public-or-sell-up-yet-i-want-to-make-multiple-small-games); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/palworld-creator-wants-to-avoid-big-budget-triple-a-games-in-the-future-as-the-hit-survival-games-profits-are-too-big-for-a-studio-with-our-size-to-handle)

### Inferences
- $700M gross over 30.5M copies implies ~$23 average gross per copy against a $29.99 list price, consistent with heavy sales in lower-priced regions (China) and periodic discounts.
- Gross revenue is roughly 100x the ~$6.7M development budget. After Steam's cut (30% falling to 20% at high volume) and taxes/refunds, net to Pocketpair is plausibly several hundred million dollars; this is my arithmetic, not a disclosed number.
- Roughly 9-10M of the 40M players never bought a copy (Game Pass), so Game Pass delivered reach and a flat fee rather than unit revenue. Xbox premium sales appear tiny (under 70k at 1.0).
- PS5 is a small slice (about 4% of players), so the business is overwhelmingly a Steam story.

### Gaps
- No official revenue or profit figure has ever been published; Pocketpair is private.
- Milestones between Feb 2024 and Jul 2026: I recall an official "32 million players" announcement around the first anniversary (Jan/Feb 2025) but did not retrieve a source in this session; verify before using.
- Could not open the Alinea report or SteamDB directly (403), so Alinea figures are via secondary summaries.
- No reliable estimate found for the Game Pass licensing fee.

## 2. Development budget, team size and development history

### Takeaway
Palworld was built for roughly 1 billion yen (~$6.7M) by a team that grew from 3-4 people to roughly 40-50, in a process Mizobe himself described as the "antithesis of proper game development". Budget was deliberately not managed; the plan was to spend until the bank account hit zero, then borrow.

### Cited Findings
- Mizobe's note.com blog post, published a few days before the Jan 2024 launch, put development cost at about 1 billion yen (~$6.75M). He said he did not want to manage a budget, and that as the bank balance approached zero he decided to borrow money. — [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/palworld-cost-one-billion-yen-to-make-and-the-ceo-didnt-want-to-budget-money/); [Game8](https://game8.co/articles/latest/palworld-cost-one-billion-yen-to-make-claims-ceo)
- It is unclear whether the "under 1 billion yen" figure quoted in March 2024 covers marketing as well as development. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/palworlds-made-so-much-money-its-too-big-for-a-studio-with-our-size-to-handle-but-ceo-says-its-not-going-to-go-public-or-sell-up-yet-i-want-to-make-multiple-small-games)
- Team growth: 3-4 members initially, growing to 20-30 in year two, with 10-20 more added in year three (no single official headcount). — [Automaton interview, 23 Jan 2024](https://automaton-media.com/en/interviews/20240123-25950/)
- Mizobe called Palworld's existence a "miracle" and the process "the antithesis of proper game development". — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/its-a-miracle-that-palworld-exists-according-to-the-studio-founder-it-was-the-antithesis-of-proper-game-development)
- Hiring story: unable to find a developer in Japan with shooter experience, Mizobe found a self-taught hobbyist on Twitter/X who was working part-time at a convenience store; that person handled guns, motion/animation and Unreal Engine scripting. — [eXputer](https://exputer.com/news/games/palworld-dev-store-worker/); [Automaton, 14 Feb 2024](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/20240214-27175/)
- Pocketpair stated the 100+ Pals are original designs, not AI-generated (response to launch accusations). — [Niche Gamer](https://nichegamer.com/palworld-developer-claims-100-pals-are-original-not-ai-generated)

### Inferences
- The low budget plus a $30 price and tens of millions of units is the core of the "why so much money" story: the margin structure of a small Japanese indie applied to AAA-scale unit volume.
- The team had prior genre practice (Craftopia, also an early-access survival-crafting game), which likely de-risked the systems design even though production was chaotic.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve the body of Mizobe's blog or the PC Gamer write-up (truncated). Details I recall from it but did not verify here: mid-development switch from Unity to Unreal Engine 4 led by one experienced engineer, a single veteran animator rescuing rigging for 100 Pals, the first trailer being announced in June 2021, and Craftopia revenue funding development. Verify before citing.
- No verified figure for Pocketpair headcount in 2026.

## 3. Pricing, business model, Game Pass and platform strategy

### Takeaway
A one-time ~$30 premium purchase with no microtransactions, launched simultaneously on Steam and Game Pass, then extended to PS5 and licensed out for mobile (Krafton, and separately Garena). 1.0 was a free update for existing owners. Nintendo platforms remain absent amid the lawsuit.

### Cited Findings
- List price $29.99 (Alinea contrasts it with $70 AAA pricing); 1.0 launched with a 30% discount on Steam and later PlayStation. — [OtakuKart summarising Alinea](https://otakukart.com/palworld-surpasses-30-5-million-copies-sold-hits-700-million-revenue-and-3-7-million-daily-players-after-version-1-0/)
- Version 1.0 was free for existing owners and released simultaneously on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Game Pass on 10 July 2026; no Switch / Switch 2 version. — [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78559-palworld-1-0-launch-date-confirmed-for-10-july); [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/149947/palworld-1-0-release-time)
- Day-one Game Pass inclusion (console, PC, cloud); Microsoft publicly said it was working with Pocketpair to support the Xbox versions. — [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2024/01/31/palworld-biggest-3rd-party-game-pass-launch-ever/)
- March 2024: Mizobe said Pocketpair was in talks to bring Palworld to more platforms and denied acquisition talks with Microsoft. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/palworld-devs-in-talks-to-bring-the-survival-game-x-pokemon-mashup-to-more-platforms-in-the-future)
- Mobile: Krafton signed a licensing deal (announced Oct 2024) for Palworld Mobile, developed under PUBG Studios; closed alpha in late 2025; Krafton targeted a 2026 launch; still in development per an Aug 2026 report, no date. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/krafton-and-pocketpair-partner-to-bring-palworld-to-mobile); [Asia Economy](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025111612012325715); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/palworld-mobile-alpha-test-set-for-q4-2025)
- A second licensed mobile title, Palworld Online (a mobile MMORPG by Garena), was announced for 2026 on iOS/Android; distinct from Krafton's Palworld Mobile. — [Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/garena-and-pocketpair-have-announced-palworld-online-a-mobile-mmorpg-launching-later-this-year-3872951); [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/palworld-online/announced-ios-android/)

### Inferences
- Pocketpair monetises the PC/console game once at a low price and pushes live-service-style monetisation risk onto licensees (Krafton, Garena), collecting licence income without building a mobile/F2P organisation.
- The $30 price sat well under AAA and near impulse-buy level for a co-op game, which lowers the friction of "all my friends are buying it".

### Gaps
- I found no source in this session confirming an explicit "no microtransactions" pledge; the game has no in-game store as far as coverage indicates, but cite carefully.
- PS5 launch date (I recall 24 Sep 2024, announced at State of Play, with the Japan release delayed), Mac release (I recall a Steam Mac version in 2025) and any Switch 2 statements were not verified in this session.
- Game Pass deal terms are undisclosed.

## 4. Why it went viral

### Takeaway
A one-line hook ("Pokemon with guns") built over two-plus years of trailers, converted at launch through Twitch and co-op word of mouth, at a low price, in a quiet January window, with controversy adding free coverage. China and the US each account for roughly a quarter to a third of Steam buyers.

### Cited Findings
- Pre-launch: the "Pokemon with guns" game was already climbing Twitch before release, with ~100,635 peak viewers on 16 Jan 2024 (creator early access). — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/cursed-pokemon-like-palworld-is-climbing-up-the-twitch-charts-and-its-not-even-officially-out-yet)
- Launch day (19 Jan 2024): 433,753 peak Twitch viewers and 4.76M hours watched. — [Streams Charts](https://streamscharts.com/games/palworld/release-stats)
- ~59 million hours watched on Twitch in January 2024, #6 category for the month (StreamElements/Rainmaker). — [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/138707/streamelements-state-of-the-stream-january-2024-palworld)
- 78 million+ hours watched on Twitch across 2024, the platform's most popular new release of the year. — [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/palworld-has-been-watched-on-twitch-for-over-78-million-hours-making-it-the-platforms-most-popular-release-of-2024/)
- Search interest for Palworld rose over 700% in January 2024. — [Tech Wire Asia](https://techwireasia.com/2024/01/video-game-industry-set-for-2024-comeback-as-palworld-searches-jump-over-700-percent/)
- 200,000 concurrent Steam players within 24 hours, building to 2.1M within about a week. — [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/palworld-hits-200000-concurrent-pc-players-less-than-24-hours-after-it-launched-in-early-access); [VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/palworld-hits-more-concurrent-steam-players-than-any-paid-game-in-history)
- Newzoo (Jan 2024): 38.5% of Palworld players had spent on Lethal Company and 36% had bought Baldur's Gate 3; among players who left other games for Palworld, 51.6% came from Fortnite. — [GameWorldObserver](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/02/21/palworld-revenue-mau-january-charts-newzoo)
- Regional (early 2024, estimate): player distribution skewed to China (~36%) and the US (~21%); a Pocketpair developer said they did not expect a third of players to be from China. — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/100868/palworld-has-absolutely-taken-over-china/index.html); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-45756)
- Regional (Alinea, Jul 2026, estimate): US ~27% and China ~27% of Steam sales; China has lower revenue per copy due to regional pricing; Simplified Chinese reviews ~92% positive. — [OtakuKart summarising Alinea](https://otakukart.com/palworld-surpasses-30-5-million-copies-sold-hits-700-million-revenue-and-3-7-million-daily-players-after-version-1-0/)
- Controversy at launch (design-similarity and AI accusations) drew a public denial from Pocketpair and extensive press. — [Niche Gamer](https://nichegamer.com/palworld-developer-claims-100-pals-are-original-not-ai-generated); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/palworld-game-pocketpair-pokemon-with-guns-1851338971)

### Inferences
- The Newzoo overlap data suggests the audience was the co-op/survival "friend-group" PC crowd (Lethal Company, Fortnite), not primarily lapsed Pokemon fans; the Pokemon framing was the marketing hook, the survival-crafting loop was the product.
- China's weight (a quarter to a third of buyers) was a major, unplanned volume driver made possible by low regional pricing on Steam.
- Game Pass plus Steam meant no platform gap in friend groups at launch, amplifying co-op word of mouth.

### Gaps
- No trailer view counts retrieved. No Japan-specific share found; Japan appears to be a minor market by units but I have no figure.
- I found no formal analyst post-mortem attributing sales to specific channels; the causal story is inference from the data above.
- The widely repeated point that Pokemon fan dissatisfaction (post Scarlet/Violet) fed demand was not sourced here.

## 5. Post-launch business: JV, publishing, lawsuit, 1.0, retention

### Takeaway
Pocketpair converted the hit into an IP business (Sony Music/Aniplex licensing JV, two mobile licences, a publishing label) while fighting a Nintendo/Pokemon Company patent suit that has forced mechanic changes but, as of mid-2026, was narrowed to old versions with a tiny damages claim. Player counts decayed to ~20k average CCU before 1.0 in July 2026 brought nearly a million back.

### Cited Findings
**Palworld Entertainment and licensing**
- 10 Jul 2024: Sony Music Entertainment (Japan), Aniplex and Pocketpair announced joint venture Palworld Entertainment, Inc. to expand the IP through licensing and merchandising outside of games; Aniplex leads merchandising. Ownership split not disclosed in sources found. — [Sony press release](https://sony.mediaroom.com/2024-07-10-Sony-Music,-Aniplex,-and-Pocketpair-Announce-Joint-Venture,-Palworld-Entertainment,-Inc-,-to-Expand-the-Breakout-Game,-Palworld); [Gematsu](https://gematsu.com/2024/07/sony-music-entertainment-japan-aniplex-and-pocketpair-jointly-establish-palworld-entertainment)

**Lawsuit (Nintendo and The Pokemon Company v. Pocketpair)**
- Filed 18 Sep 2024 in Tokyo District Court, announced 19 Sep 2024; patent infringement, not copyright. — [Nintendo release](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/corporate/release/en/2024/240919.html); [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2024/11/pocketpair-details-lawsuit-filed-against-it-by-nintendo-and-the-pokemon-company)
- Pocketpair disclosure, 8 Nov 2024: three Japanese patents (Nos. 7545191, 7493117, 7528390), all applied for Feb-Jul 2024 and registered May-Aug 2024 (after Palworld's launch, as divisionals with 2021 priority); plaintiffs seek an injunction plus 5 million yen each (10 million yen total, roughly $60-65k) plus late-payment damages. — [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2024/11/pocketpair-details-lawsuit-filed-against-it-by-nintendo-and-the-pokemon-company); [games fray](https://gamesfray.com/ahead-of-october-1-court-hearing-nintendo-has-zero-chance-of-prevailing-over-current-palworld-versions-it-may-get-30k-chump-change/)
- Mechanic changes confirmed by Pocketpair (statement 8 May 2025): patch v0.3.11 (30 Nov 2024) removed summoning Pals by throwing Pal Spheres, replaced with a static summon beside the player; patch v0.5.5 (May 2025) changed gliding to require a glider item instead of using Pals directly (Pals give a passive buff). Pocketpair called the changes disappointing but necessary to avoid further disruption to development. — [Pocketpair statement](https://pocketpair.jp/en/news/regarding-the-lawsuit-changes-to-palworld-and-the-future/); [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/palworld-confirms-disappointing-game-changes-forced-by-pokemon-lawsuit)
- Nov 2025: plaintiffs amended the complaint so it covers only older Palworld versions released before the features were removed. — [games fray, 11 Jun 2026](https://gamesfray.com/ahead-of-october-1-court-hearing-nintendo-has-zero-chance-of-prevailing-over-current-palworld-versions-it-may-get-30k-chump-change/)
- Analyst opinion (Florian Mueller, games fray, 11 Jun 2026): no realistic path for an injunction against current versions including 1.0; maximum recovery estimated at about JPY 5 million (~$30k). Schedule: evidence presentation 1 Oct 2026; court to express its opinion 9 Nov 2026. This is commentary, not a ruling. — [games fray](https://gamesfray.com/ahead-of-october-1-court-hearing-nintendo-has-zero-chance-of-prevailing-over-current-palworld-versions-it-may-get-30k-chump-change/)
- Patent-office setbacks for Nintendo in 2026: USPTO issued a non-final rejection of all 26 claims of a US "summon sub-character and battle" patent (reported as US 12,433,397; dated March or 1 April 2026 depending on source); the Japan Patent Office rejected a related monster-capture application (reported 24 Apr 2026) citing prior art. These are not the patents-in-suit decisions and do not bind the Tokyo court. — [Technobezz](https://www.technobezz.com/news/us-patent-office-rejects-nintendos-26-claims-for-summon-subcharacter-patent); [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/nintendos-latest-palworld-patent-attempt-hits-a-dead-end-in-japan)
- Outcome status as of 14 Nov 2026: I found no reporting on the 1 Oct hearing or the 9 Nov court opinion; no judgment or confirmed settlement found. One low-quality site claims the case "settled", contradicted by all other sources. — [lawfold (aggregator)](https://lawfold.com/palworld-nintendo-pokemon-lawsuit/); [games fray](https://gamesfray.com/ahead-of-october-1-court-hearing-nintendo-has-zero-chance-of-prevailing-over-current-palworld-versions-it-may-get-30k-chump-change/)

**1.0 release**
- Date confirmed at Summer Game Fest 2026: 10 July 2026, ending ~2.5 years of early access; added 72 new Pals (World Tree content). — [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78559-palworld-1-0-launch-date-confirmed-for-10-july); [OtakuKart](https://otakukart.com/palworld-surpasses-30-5-million-copies-sold-hits-700-million-revenue-and-3-7-million-daily-players-after-version-1-0/)
- Steam CCU went from ~58,000 to nearly a million within days; reported peaks 855,525 (first weekend) and 956,613 on the second weekend (about 20 Jul 2026), #2 on Steam behind Counter-Strike 2 and the game's second-highest peak ever. — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/112791/palworlds-1-0-launch-pulled-its-concurrent-player-count-from-58000-to-nearly-a-million-in-a-matter-of-days/index.html); [MassivelyOP, 21 Jul 2026](https://massivelyop.com/2026/07/21/palworld-has-now-hit-nearly-a-million-concurrent-players-since-its-1-0-launch/); [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/palworld-855000-players-second-highest-peak/)

**Retention and update-driven spikes (Steam, tracker data)**
- Feybreak update, Dec 2024: peak 212,623 CCU. — [GameCharts](https://gamecharts.org/steam/palworld)
- Tides of Terraria crossover, late Jun 2025: peak ~122,500 CCU, back in Steam's top 10; community manager joked it would be branded a "dead game" again within a week. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/palworlds-terraria-update-lands-it-back-in-steams-top-10-with-over-120-000-players-coming-back-but-its-community-manager-is-just-waiting-for-people-to-brand-it-a-dead-game-again)
- Decay between updates: average CCU 33,076 in Aug 2025, 25,177 in Sep 2025, and a low of 20,084 average (28,705 peak) in May 2026 before 1.0. — [GameCharts](https://gamecharts.org/steam/palworld)

### Inferences
- The lawsuit's direct financial exposure (10 million yen claimed) is trivial relative to ~$700M gross; its real costs were design compromises, development distraction, and probably the absence of any Nintendo-platform release.
- Palworld behaves like a premium "content drop" game, not a live service: 99% CCU decay from peak is normal for it and does not hurt revenue because there is nothing recurring to sell; each major update re-activates owners and sells an incremental tranche (1.0: ~1.5M Steam copies, about 5% of lifetime).
- The 1.0 spike (nearly half the all-time peak, 2.5 years later) shows an unusually large dormant owner base willing to return.

## 6. Leadership on acquisition offers and staying small

### Takeaway
Mizobe's public position is "we are and will remain a small studio" making multiple small games, not AAA. In March 2024 he said he was open to considering partnership or acquisition offers; by 2025 the communications director said Mizobe "would never allow" an acquisition. I found no sourced account of specific offers being turned down.

- Same interview: open to considering offers for partnership or acquisition, but had not engaged in acquisition talks with Microsoft. — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/?p=581777); [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/articles/palworld-developer-responds-to-microsoft-acquisition-rumors/1100-6521872/)
- John Buckley (communications director / publishing head), 2025: "Our CEO would never allow it"; an acquisition would likely only happen once Mizobe starts thinking about retirement; a sale is highly unlikely. Coverage notes this runs counter to Mizobe's March 2024 openness. — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/palworld-studios-ceo-would-never-allow-an-acquisition-communications-director); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-47549)
- Game8 summary of the studio's stance: "bigger is not better", content to remain an indie studio. — [Game8](https://game8.co/articles/latest/palworld-devs-believe-bigger-is-not-better-and-remains-content-as-indie-game-studio)

### Inferences
- Rather than selling equity, Pocketpair took strategic partners at the IP level (Sony Music/Aniplex JV, Krafton and Garena licences), which gives big-company reach without ceding control of the studio.
- Stance hardened from "open to offers" (Mar 2024) to "never" (2025), plausibly because the cash position removed any need for a buyer.
